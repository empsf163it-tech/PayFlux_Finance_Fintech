# PayFlux — Institutional Finance & Global FinTech Platform

PayFlux is an institutional corporate finance and modern fintech platform powering cross-border commercial banking, multi-currency treasury yield, corporate expense card programs, and interbank foreign exchange liquidity.

---

## Financial Platform Architecture (6 Pages)

1. **`index.html`** (Main Financial Platform Overview)
   - Institutional value proposition hero with interactive 3D commercial Visa card and real-time T+0 clearing ledger.
   - Global banking & compliance strip (Visa, Mastercard, SWIFT Clearing, Tier 1 Custody, PCI-DSS L1, AML/KYC).
   - Live foreign exchange (FX) converter and legacy commercial bank wire fee comparison.
   - Core 4-pillar financial suite (Cross-Border Banking, Commercial Cards, Merchant Acquiring, Treasury & Yield).
   - Interactive Institutional Treasury & Liquidity Desk (FX Liquidity, Sovereign Treasury Yield, Corporate Card Ledger).
   - CFO and Corporate Treasurer client impact stories and consultation call to action.

2. **`about.html`** (About PayFlux & Institutional Governance)
   - Corporate financial mandate and elimination of correspondent banking friction.
   - Fiduciary principles: Capital Efficiency, Fiduciary Rigor, Zero Hidden Margins, and Regulatory Excellence.
   - Segregated Tier 1 bank custody safeguards and global financial clearing hubs (New York, London, Singapore, Tokyo).
   - Executive leadership governance across global transaction banking, treasury FX, compliance, and commercial cards.

3. **`services.html`** (Banking & FinTech Services)
   - **Cross-Border Corporate Banking**: T+0 settlement across 140+ countries (SEPA Instant, Fedwire, ACH, Faster Payments, Pix, UPI).
   - **Commercial & Virtual Corporate Cards**: Multi-currency card issuing, pre-spend budget governance, VAT receipt matching, and 1.5% cashback rebates.
   - **Merchant Payment Acquiring**: Global payment acceptance, 3D Secure 2 protection, and chargeback shield.
   - **Multi-Currency IBANs & Sovereign Sweeps**: Named corporate IBANs and automated U.S. Treasury Bill sweeps.
   - 4-step institutional KYB/AML onboarding workflow.

4. **`solutions.html`** (Treasury, Yield & Capital Solutions)
   - Three pillars of corporate capital efficiency: Sovereign Yield Sweeps (up to 4.95% APY), FX Forward Risk Hedging, and Net-60 Working Capital Credit Lines.
   - Interactive Institutional Portfolio & Treasury Desk (Treasury Portfolio, FX Forward Desk, Credit & Float).
   - Tailored financial verticals: Global Trade & Commerce, Holding Groups & Funds, Marketplaces & Escrow, and ERP/Tax Accounting Sync (NetSuite, SAP, Xero, QuickBooks).

5. **`pricing.html`** (Transparent Pricing, Interchange & FX Rates)
   - Interactive monthly payment & FX volume calculator modeling blended rates and annual fee savings vs. legacy banks.
   - Three corporate account tiers: **Commercial**, **Corporate Treasury**, and **Institutional**.
   - Financial custody, foreign exchange, KYB onboarding, and multi-currency card FAQs.

6. **`contact.html`** (Institutional Finance Advisory & Global Banking Hubs)
   - Corporate financial consultation request form for CFOs and finance teams.
   - Direct relationship banking, 24/7 treasury & cardholders desk, and custodial compliance channels.
   - Global financial district headquarters directory (Wall Street, Canary Wharf, Marina Bay) and interactive location map.
